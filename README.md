## About the course

The connected activities develop an annual WSH refresher for **HarbourLink Logistics (fictional)**, from storyboard to accessible interactions, graded quiz, review and LMS/LRS publishing. The audience is frontline warehouse operators, including staff with low reading confidence: use pictures, short labels, plain-English narration, captions and replay controls. Authentic Storyline interface screenshots sit alongside the instructions, with clearly labelled AI recreated illustrations for visual orientation. The final activity is an independent project: learners choose their own organisation or learning topic and create an original e-learning module.

## Learning outcomes

- Conceptualise and map digital storyboards integrating storytelling principles in Storyline 360.
- Identify and determine audience-fit e-learning content, types, styles and features.
- Determine effective modes and processes for distributing interactive e-learning content.
- Develop execution guidelines and determine content-delivery frequency.

## Topics covered

| Topic | Applied Storyline features |
|---|---|
| Project and storyboard | Story View, scenes, slides, layouts, themes, masters, object naming, Timeline and project variables |
| Media and interaction | Pictures, Content Library, hotspots, markers, layers, states, triggers, motion, sliders, dials, screen-recording simulations, audio and captions |
| Decisions and quizzing | Branching, freeform interactions, graded questions, question banks, results, retry, review and completion logic |
| Review and delivery | Player, accessibility, translation, Review 360, SCORM 1.2/2004, xAPI (Tin Can API), cmi5 context and LMS/LRS test evidence |

## Mock assets and use

The [shared mock assets](activities/source/) include hazard and safe-comparison scenes, storyboard, quiz content, style guide, eight narration clips with matching captions, a full computer-generated voice-over track, and a local incident-report form for screen-recording practice.

[Download the narrated HarbourLink WSH example · MP4](activities/source/video/HarbourLink-Annual-WSH-Refresher.mp4) · [Captions · VTT](activities/source/captions/harbourlink-annual-wsh.vtt) · [Video player and download page](activities/source/video/index.html). The 3-minute-22-second video is a linear narrated companion; scored quizzes and clickable hotspots are built in the interactive Storyline module. Each Markdown guide has a same-folder PDF copy.

Use Storyline 360 on Windows with an appropriate licence. Start from a blank project and carry your own checkpoints forward. Completed `.story` solutions and pre-published Storyline SCORM/xAPI packages are not supplied. Publish and validate your own outputs in an approved LMS/LRS sandbox; a browser preview alone does not prove tracking compatibility. Experimental Articulate Labs features are optional sandbox exploration.
