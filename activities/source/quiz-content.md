# Workplace Safety Quiz Content

Use these five fictional practice questions in Activity 09. When deploying your own module, obtain approval against the real employer policy before release.

1. **Liquid spill:** What is the safest immediate response? Correct: isolate the area and report the spill. Distractors: walk through carefully; wait for someone else. Feedback: isolation prevents exposure while the approved response is arranged.
2. **Emergency exit:** Which control keeps the exit usable? Correct: remove the obstruction and keep the marked route clear. Distractors: add a warning sign only; lock the door. Feedback: the route and door must remain immediately usable.
3. **Unstable pallet — select all that apply:** Correct: stop access; use approved handling equipment; escalate to the supervisor. Distractors: climb the stack; pull the lowest carton. Feedback: do not enter the collapse zone or destabilise the load.
4. **Near miss:** What is the correct fictional reporting route? Correct: make the area safe, notify the supervisor and submit the training incident report. Distractors: post to social media; wait for an injury. Feedback: prompt internal reporting enables corrective action.
5. **Loading bay — select all that apply:** Correct: required PPE, marked pedestrian route and active traffic separation. Distractors: headphones; unmarked shortcut. Feedback: personal protection and traffic controls work together.

The classroom quiz uses five questions and an 80% pass threshold. The approved formal PP remains a separate four-task, 60-minute instrument and retains its required assessment specifications.
